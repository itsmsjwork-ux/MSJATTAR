About the brand

Mohammed Shahid Joshiddi’s Fragrance House

MSJ Attar honors the traditional art of concentrated perfume oil: patient blending, elegant restraint, and a respect for botanicals that have scented gatherings, prayer, celebration, and personal ritual for generations.

Founded by Mohammed Shahid Joshiddi, the brand brings a premium lens to classic attar culture, pairing hand-selected notes with modern presentation and a commitment to alcohol-free refinement.

Heritage Traditional attar craft and regional fragrance memory.
Purity Oil-based blends with thoughtful ingredient selection.
Exclusivity Small-batch releases for collectors and connoisseurs.
